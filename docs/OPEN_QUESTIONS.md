## OQ-002 - R2 nen mo he thong nao?

**Question:** Sau khi Day 1 dat playtest, lat cat tiep theo nen la gi?

**Goal:** Tang chieu sau ma khong xay dong thoi ca simulation lon.

**Constraints:** Moi lat cat phai playtest va reset nhanh.

**Options:**

1. O nha hay ra ngoai trong mot ngay co quy thoi gian.
2. Chuan bi hanh trang va trade-off bao ho/suc chua.
3. O nhiem be mat, kho sach/cach ly va khu nhiem loot.
4. Mot ngay bao van hanh shelter.

**Recommendation:** R2 nen la `O nha hay ra ngoai`.

**Why:** No mo rong truc quyet dinh chinh cua baseline ma van gan truc tiep voi Day 1.

**Risks:** Neu HUD/onboarding R1 chua ro, them time economy co the lam nguoi choi roi.

**Prototype:** Mot ngay co ETA, viec nha, chuyen di gan/trung/xa va summary so sanh co hoi loot voi tien do shelter bi mat.

**Decision status:** Proposed.

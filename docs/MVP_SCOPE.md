# MVP Scope

## Cau hoi MVP phai tra loi

**Fact:** MVP hien tai khong phai game sinh ton hoan chinh. No la prototype de kiem chung vong lap:

> nhan tin hieu -> ra quyet dinh -> roi shelter -> thu thap -> quay ve -> chuan bi -> thay hau qua.

## Pham vi da duyet cho rebuild

**Decision (`DEC-011`):** Bat dau lai bang Day 1, cold open trong shelter.

MVP rebuild hien tai gom:

- Mot nhan vat.
- Mot shelter.
- Mot khu ngoai troi nho.
- Hai diem loot tao lua chon gan/an toan va xa/loi nhieu hon.
- Thoi gian va lieu phong xa tang khi o ngoai shelter.
- Mot nut tuong tac.
- Mot summary cuoi ngay.
- Reset nhanh de playtest lai.

## Muc tieu Day 1

**Decision:** Day 1 phai day du core loop bang hanh dong trong game, khong dua vao giai thich mieng.

Nguoi choi can tu hieu:

- Bao phong xa dang den.
- Shelter chua san sang.
- Filter la nhu cau dau tien.
- Ra ngoai co loi nhung co rui ro.
- Di xa co the dem them material nhung ton thoi gian va lieu.
- Quay ve dung loot moi hoan tat ngay.

## Thanh cong cua R1

Day 1 duoc coi la dat khi:

- 4/5 nguoi moi tu roi shelter trong 90 giay.
- 4/5 nguoi moi noi dung rang bao dang den va shelter can chuan bi.
- It nhat 3/5 nguoi can nhac di tiep sau loot dau tien.
- Sau ngay 1, nguoi choi ke lai duoc mot trade-off giua loot, lieu va thoi gian.

## Ngoai pham vi MVP rebuild

- Khong build lai six-cycle run cu.
- Khong inventory grid.
- Khong crafting tree day du.
- Khong shelter module hoan chinh truoc khi Day 1 dat.
- Khong final fallout dai.
- Khong minimap, fog-of-war, building interiors hoac world expansion trong R1.
- Khong combat hoac enemy AI.

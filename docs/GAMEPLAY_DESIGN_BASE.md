# Gameplay Design Base - Bao Phong Xa

## Trang thai

**Decision (`DEC-010`):** Tai lieu nay la product design baseline cho Last Hope.

**Decision (`DEC-011`):** Baseline nay dinh huong san pham dai han, nhung implementation hien tai bat dau lai bang `DayOne`. Khong mac dinh toan bo he thong duoi day thuoc R1.

## Fantasy gameplay

Nguoi choi song trong mot shelter tam bo sau khi he thong canh bao cho biet bao phong xa co the den trong vai ngay. Moi ngay la mot cau hoi kho: ra ngoai tim them thu can thiet, hay o nha sua shelter va bao toan suc khoe?

## Cau hoi trung tam

> Hom nay toi nen mao hiem them bao nhieu de tang co hoi song sot khi bao den?

## Tru cot gameplay

1. **Rui ro doc duoc:** nguoi choi thay thoi gian, lieu phong xa, khoang cach va loi ich truoc khi quyet dinh.
2. **Khong the chuan bi hoan hao:** moi hanh dong co chi phi; di ra ngoai, o nha, dung ngay hay de danh deu can can nhac.
3. **Thong tin thay doi ke hoach:** radio, ban do, may do va quan sat lam ke hoach tot hon nhung khong bien game thanh checklist.
4. **Shelter bien loot thanh song sot:** loot chi co y nghia khi duoc xu ly, du tru, sua chua hoac xay dung.
5. **Bao tra lai hau qua:** giai doan bao phai cho thay lua chon nao giup, lua chon nao gay thieu hut.

## Cau truc mot run muc tieu

### Giai doan 1 - Chuan bi

6-8 ngay. Moi ngay nguoi choi nhan tin hieu, chon o nha hoac ra ngoai, chuan bi hanh trang, di/loot/quay ve, xu ly loot va dung thoi gian con lai o shelter.

### Giai doan 2 - Canh bao cuoi

Nguoi choi chot chuyen di cuoi, hoan thien sua chua, xu ly nhiem ban, chuyen kho va dong shelter. Du bao co sai so nho de tao ap luc, nhung khong gay chet bat ngo khong the suy luan.

### Giai doan 3 - Bao

2-4 ngay. Kham pha tu do dung lai. Nguoi choi van hanh shelter: do kin, loc khi, dien/nhien lieu, nuoc/thuc an, nhiem ban va su co.

## Lat cat hien tai

**Fact:** Implementation hien tai chi la `DayOne`.

`DayOne` can chung minh:

- Nguoi choi hieu bao dang den.
- Nguoi choi hieu shelter thieu filter.
- Nguoi choi tu roi shelter.
- Nguoi choi thay trade-off giua loot gan va loot xa.
- Nguoi choi quay ve va thay loot co tac dung.

## He thong muc tieu sau R1

- Time budget theo ngay.
- Viec o nha canh tranh voi ra ngoai.
- Hanh trang va bao ho canh tranh suc chua.
- Loot sach/nhiem/cach ly.
- Shelter co do kin, loc khi, dien, sua chua va kho.
- Bao co su co voi 2-3 lua chon hop ly.
- Summary giai thich nguyen nhan ket qua.

## Ranh gioi scope

**Decision:** Chi mo he thong moi sau khi lat cat hien tai tao them cau hoi playtest ro rang.

**Ngoai pham vi hien tai:** combat hoan chinh, enemy AI phuc tap, multiplayer, procedural world, vehicle, faction/colony, meta-progression va nhieu loai disaster.
